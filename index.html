Roof replacement

Plan a replacement with clear communication at each stage.

Signs to discuss replacement

  • Widespread wear, curling or granule loss
  • Repeated repairs in different areas
  • An older roof showing multiple issues
  • Extensive storm damage

Planning and communication

We discuss goals, materials and project sequence, and keep you informed as plans take shape. Timelines depend on the project and are confirmed in writing, if offered.

What affects project scope

  • Roof size and pitch
  • Chosen materials
  • Condition of the existing structure
  • Access, layers and details such as vents or skylights

No prices are listed. Scope is discussed during your estimate.
